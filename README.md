## 👋 About Me

Hey! I'm **Kyro** (`@its_me_kyro`), a curious tech enthusiast who enjoys exploring technology, experimenting with ideas, and understanding how things work.

I'm still at the beginning of my programming journey. I have basic knowledge of several languages and I'm continuously learning, experimenting, and improving my skills.

- 💻 Learning **JavaScript, Python & TypeScript**.
- 🤖 Exploring **Discord.js** and Discord bot development.
- 🛡️ Currently working as a **Discord Community Manager**.
- 🎮 I enjoy playing **Among Us, Super Sus**, and other multiplayer games.
- 🔧 Interested in software, open-source projects, and community management.
- 🐧 Planning to explore **CachyOS** in the future.
- 📚 Education comes first, so my activity may be limited sometimes.
- 🌱 Always curious about something new.
